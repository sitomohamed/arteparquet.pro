## Stage 1: Awareness

**Mindset:** "I need new flooring / My floor needs work"

### Triggers
- Renovation project starting
- Moving to new home
- Damaged/worn flooring
- Desire for upgrade
- Architect recommendation

### Channels
- Google search
- Social media inspiration
- Word of mouth
- Architect/designer referral
- Seeing a beautiful floor somewhere

### Website Touchpoints
- Homepage (first impression)
- Google search result (title + meta)
- Social media posts

### Content Needs
- Inspiration (beautiful images)
- Awareness of options
- General education

### Actions to Take
- [ ] SEO optimized for discovery keywords
- [ ] Strong social media presence
- [ ] Clear value proposition on homepage
- [ ] Fast, beautiful first impression

---

## Stage 2: Interest

**Mindset:** "Who can help me? What are my options?"

### Activities
- Browsing multiple websites
- Comparing services
- Looking at portfolios
- Reading about materials
- Checking locations served

### Website Touchpoints
- Services pages
- Portfolio overview
- About page preview
- Blog articles (if searching for info)

### Content Needs
- Clear service explanations
- Visual proof of quality
- Geographic coverage info
- Pricing indicators

### Emotions
- Curiosity
- Excitement about possibilities
- Some overwhelm with options

### Actions to Take
- [ ] Clear service categorization
- [ ] Compelling portfolio with variety
- [ ] Easy navigation between services
- [ ] "Operiamo in tutta Italia" visible
- [ ] Engaging service descriptions

---

## Stage 3: Consideration

**Mindset:** "Can I trust them? Are they right for me?"

### Activities
- Deep dive into portfolio
- Reading testimonials
- Checking credentials
- Comparing 2-3 options
- Looking for red flags
- Discussing with family/partners

### Website Touchpoints
- Portfolio detail pages
- Testimonials section
- About page (La Scala story)
- FAQ page
- Contact page (before submitting)

### Content Needs
- Detailed project information
- Social proof
- Credentials and experience
- Clear process explanation
- Answers to common questions

### Emotions
- Evaluating trust
- Seeking reassurance
- Fear of wrong choice
- Hope for transformation

### Key Trust Signals
1. **La Scala credential** - Ultimate authority
2. **20+ years experience** - Longevity
3. **Testimonials** - Social proof
4. **Portfolio quality** - Visual proof
5. **Transparent process** - No surprises

### Actions to Take
- [ ] La Scala story prominently featured
- [ ] Testimonials with names and context
- [ ] Detailed portfolio with stories
- [ ] Clear process explanation
- [ ] FAQ addressing common concerns
- [ ] Professional, premium design

---

## Stage 4: Decision

**Mindset:** "I'm ready to reach out"

### Activities
- Filling out contact form
- Calling or WhatsApp
- Requesting quote
- Scheduling consultation

### Website Touchpoints
- Contact form (multi-step)
- WhatsApp button
- Phone click-to-call
- Thank you page

### Content Needs
- Easy form to complete
- Clear expectations after submission
- Reassurance of response time
- Alternative contact methods

### Emotions
- Commitment anxiety
- Excitement
- Impatience for response
- Hope

### Conversion Optimization
- [ ] Multi-step form (reduces friction)
- [ ] Progress indicator
- [ ] Clear CTA buttons
- [ ] Multiple contact options
- [ ] Form field validation
- [ ] Thank you page with next steps
- [ ] Auto-response email

---

## Stage 5: Post-Contact (Off-Site)

**Mindset:** "Did I make the right choice?"

### Activities
- Waiting for response
- Preparing questions
- Reviewing information received
- Site visit/consultation
- Receiving quote
- Making final decision
- Project execution

### Touchpoints
- Email responses
- Phone calls
- Site visit
- Quote document
- Contract

### Content Needs
- Professional email communication
- Clear quote document
- Project timeline
- Contract clarity

### Emotions
- Anticipation
- Validation seeking
- Budget consideration
- Excitement as project starts

### Actions to Take
- [ ] Quick response (< 24 hours)
- [ ] Professional quote format
- [ ] Clear communication throughout
- [ ] Regular project updates

---

## Stage 6: Loyalty & Advocacy

**Mindset:** "I love my new floor / I'd recommend them"

### Activities
- Enjoying completed project
- Showing to friends/family
- Writing reviews
- Referring others
- Future maintenance needs

### Touchpoints
- Follow-up email/call
- Review request
- Social media sharing
- Future project inquiries
- Maintenance services

### Content Needs
- Care/maintenance guide
- Easy review submission
- Social sharing prompts
- Newsletter (optional)

### Actions to Take
- [ ] Post-project follow-up
- [ ] Review request email
- [ ] Maintenance guide delivery
- [ ] Social media share request
- [ ] Referral program (future)

## Touchpoint Optimization

### High-Impact Moments

| Moment | Impact | Optimization |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| First website visit | Critical | Fast, beautiful, clear |
| Portfolio viewing | High | Quality images, stories |
| La Scala discovery | High | Prominent, credible |
| Form submission | Critical | Easy, guided |
| First response | Critical | Fast, professional |
| Quote delivery | High | Clear, detailed |
| Project completion | High | Exceed expectations |

### Friction Points to Eliminate

| Friction | Solution |
|----------|----------|
| Slow page load | Performance optimization |
| Complex navigation | Clear IA, breadcrumbs |
| Long forms | Multi-step, progressive |
| Unclear pricing | Pricing indications |
| No response | Quick turnaround |
| Uncertainty | Clear process, updates |
