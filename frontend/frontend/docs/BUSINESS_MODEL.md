# Business Model

## Arteparquet Business Model Canvas

### Overview

Arteparquet operates as a premium B2C and B2B service provider in the wood flooring industry, focusing on high-value projects with exceptional quality standards.

---

## Business Model Canvas

### 1. Value Propositions

| Proposition | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **La Scala Credentials** | Proven excellence at Italy's most prestigious venue |
| **20+ Years Experience** | Decades of refined craftsmanship |
| **Nationwide Coverage** | Service across all of Italy |
| **Premium Quality** | Only the finest materials and techniques |
| **Full Service** | From consultation to installation to maintenance |
| **Free Inspections** | No-commitment site assessments |
| **Transparent Pricing** | Detailed, honest quotations |

### 2. Customer Segments

#### Primary Segments

| Segment | Characteristics | Value |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Affluent Homeowners** | High income, quality-focused, renovation projects | High (€5,000-50,000+) |
| **Luxury Villas** | Premium properties, large areas, demanding standards | Very High (€20,000-100,000+) |
| **Architects** | Professional partners, multiple projects, specifications | High (recurring) |
| **Interior Designers** | Collaborative projects, aesthetic focus | Medium-High |
| **Hotels** | Large scale, durability needs, brand image | Very High |
| **Restaurants** | Ambiance critical, heavy traffic, quick turnaround | Medium-High |

#### Secondary Segments

| Segment | Characteristics | Value |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Construction Companies** | Subcontracting, volume projects | Medium (volume) |
| **Commercial Offices** | Professional spaces, standard specs | Medium |
| **Real Estate Developers** | New builds, multiple units | High (volume) |

| Stream | Type | Pricing Model |
|--------|------|---------------|

| **Floor Restoration** | Primary | Per m² + condition |
| **Sanding & Refinishing** | Primary | Per m² |

| **Skirting Boards** | Secondary | Per linear meter |
| **Consultation** | Tertiary | Free (lead generation) |
| **Maintenance Plans** | Tertiary | Annual subscription |

#### Installation Pricing (Indicative)

| Service | Price Range (€/m²) | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|-------|

| Solid Wood (Massello) | €80-150+ | Premium |
