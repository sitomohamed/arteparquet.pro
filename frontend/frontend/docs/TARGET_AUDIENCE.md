# Target Audience

## Arteparquet Target Market Analysis

### Overview

This document defines and analyzes the target audiences for Arteparquet, enabling precise messaging, design decisions, and marketing strategies.

---

## Primary Audiences

### 1. Affluent Homeowners

**Demographics:**
- Age: 40-65
- Household income: €70,000-200,000+
- Location: Major Italian cities, suburbs
- Property: Own home, 100-300mq

**Characteristics:**
- Quality over price mindset
- Research-oriented decision making
- Value aesthetics and craftsmanship
- Willing to invest in home
- Often working with designers

**Motivations:**
- Create beautiful living space
- Investment in property value
- Pride in home appearance
- Comfort and warmth

**Pain Points:**
- Fear of wrong choice
- Finding reliable contractors
- Disruption to daily life
- Understanding options

**Where They Are:**
- Google searching
- Houzz, Pinterest
- Design magazines
- Word of mouth

### 2. Architects & Interior Designers

**Demographics:**
- Age: 30-55
- Professional studio owners/employees
- Based in major cities
- Managing multiple projects

**Characteristics:**
- Time-constrained
- Detail-oriented
- Reputation-conscious
- Need reliable partners
- Technical knowledge

**Motivations:**
- Execute client vision perfectly
- Build reliable contractor network
- Maintain professional reputation
- Efficient collaboration

**Pain Points:**
- Unreliable subcontractors
- Quality inconsistency
- Communication issues
- Timeline problems

**Where They Are:**
- Professional networks
- Industry events
- Colleague referrals
- Specialized searches

### 3. Luxury Property Owners

**Demographics:**
- Age: 50-70
- Net worth: €2M+
- Multiple properties
- Often delegating decisions

**Characteristics:**
- Expect the absolute best
- Price is secondary
- Status-conscious
- Demand exclusivity
- Value discretion

**Motivations:**
- Befitting their success
- Lasting quality
- Best materials
- White-glove service

**Pain Points:**
- Finding worthy craftsmen
- Maintaining privacy
- Coordinating complex projects
- Getting true quality

**Where They Are:**
- Architect referrals
- Elite networks
- Property managers
- Word of mouth

### 4. Hospitality Owners

**Demographics:**
- Hotel/restaurant owners
- 40-60 years old
- Business-focused
- ROI-oriented

**Characteristics:**
- Brand-conscious
- Need durability + aesthetics
- Time-sensitive projects
- Budget accountability

**Motivations:**
- Impress guests
- Durability under traffic
- Brand alignment
- Minimal downtime

**Pain Points:**
- Revenue loss during work
- Need for quick turnaround
- Finding right aesthetic
- Maintenance concerns

**Where They Are:**
- Industry contacts
- Trade publications
- Contractor networks
- Online search

## Secondary Audiences

### 5. Construction Companies

**Role:** Subcontracting relationship

**Needs:**
- Reliable scheduling
- Consistent quality
- Competitive pricing
- Professional conduct

### 6. Real Estate Developers

**Role:** New builds and renovations

**Needs:**
- Volume capacity
- Quality consistency
- Timeline reliability
- Cost efficiency
