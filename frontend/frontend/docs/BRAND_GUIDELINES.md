# Brand Guidelines

## Arteparquet Brand Identity

### Brand Essence

**"L'Arte del Legno"** — The Art of Wood

Arteparquet is not a flooring company. It is an atelier of wood artisans who transform spaces into timeless masterpieces.

## Brand Attributes

### Core Values

| Value | Meaning | Expression |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Excellence** | Uncompromising quality in every detail | La Scala-level standards |
| **Craftsmanship** | Artisanal skill passed through generations | Hand-finished perfection |
| **Trust** | Reliability, transparency, honesty | Clear communication, no surprises |

| **Italian Heritage** | Pride in Italian design tradition | Elegance, sophistication |

### Brand Personality

If Arteparquet were a person:

- **Confident** but not arrogant
- **Expert** but approachable
- **Refined** but warm
- **Traditional** but progressive
- **Italian** in soul and execution

### Tone of Voice

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Speak with quiet confidence | Boast or oversell |
| Use elegant, refined language | Use generic corporate speak |
| Be warm and personal | Be cold or robotic |
| Explain with expertise | Overwhelm with jargon |
| Inspire with storytelling | List features without context |

## Logo Specifications

### Primary Logo

**Icon**: A circular badge containing the letter "A"
- The "A" uses the primary brand color (Rovere/Oak)
- The circle suggests completeness, perfection, unity
- Clean, modern serif or elegant sans-serif "A"

**Wordmark**: "Arteparquet.pro"
- Set in the primary heading font
- All lowercase except "A" (or all lowercase for modernity)
- Clean letter spacing

**Tagline**: "Specialisti in Parquet • SPC • PVC • Laminati"
- Set below the wordmark
- Smaller size, lighter weight
- Separated by bullet points (•)

### Logo Clear Space

Minimum clear space around logo = height of the "A" icon

### Logo Minimum Size

- Digital: 120px wide (horizontal lockup)
- Print: 30mm wide (horizontal lockup)
- Icon only: 24px minimum

### Logo Misuse

Never:
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Change the colors outside brand palette
- Add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
- Place on busy backgrounds
- Rotate the logo
- Rearrange elements

## Brand Elements

### The "A" Badge

The circular "A" badge is a versatile brand element:

- Use as a favicon
- Use as a profile picture
- Use as a watermark on images
- Use as a decorative element

### Pattern Element

A subtle herringbone or chevron pattern inspired by parquet can be used:

- As background textures (very subtle, 3-5% opacity)
- As decorative dividers
- In promotional materials

### Photography Style

| Subject | Style |
|---------|-------|
| **Finished Floors** | Wide angle, natural light, warm tones, lifestyle context |
| **Details** | Macro shots, wood grain, craftsmanship details |
| **Process** | Artisan hands at work, tools, dedication |
| **Portraits** | Arabi Mohamed, confident, professional, warm |
| **Spaces** | Elegant interiors, aspirational living |

### Imagery Guidelines

- Warm, natural color grading
- Emphasis on texture and materiality
- Human presence when appropriate (hands, silhouettes)
- Italian aesthetic (design-forward spaces)
- No stock photo "smiles" or staged scenes
- Authentic, editorial quality

## Brand Voice Examples

### Headlines

✅ Do:
- "L'eccellenza scolpita nel legno"
- "Dal palcoscenico della Scala alla tua casa"
- "Vent'anni di arte, sotto i tuoi piedi"

❌ Don't:
- "Best Parquet Services in Italy"
- "Cheap and Fast Floor Installation"
- "Click Here for a Free Quote!!!"

### Body Copy

✅ Do:
> "Every plank of wood tells a story. Our craft is to orchestrate these stories into a symphony beneath your feet. With the same precision demanded by the stages of La Scala, we transform your space into a timeless work of art."

❌ Don't:
> "We are a leading parquet company offering a wide range of flooring services. Our experienced team provides quality installation at competitive prices. Contact us today!"

### CTAs

✅ Do:
- "Richiedi una consulenza"
- "Scopri le nostre realizzazioni"
- "Raccontaci il tuo progetto"

❌ Don't:
- "CLICK HERE NOW!"
- "Get Free Quote"
- "Submit"

## Brand Governance

### Consistency Rules

1. Always use approved brand assets
2. Maintain clear space requirements
3. Use only brand colors
4. Follow typography guidelines
5. Apply photography standards
6. Write in the brand voice

### Brand Asset Management

All brand assets should be stored in:
- `/public/brand/` - Digital assets
- Cloud storage - Source files
- Brand portal - Team access

### Review Process

All external-facing materials should be reviewed for brand consistency before publication.
