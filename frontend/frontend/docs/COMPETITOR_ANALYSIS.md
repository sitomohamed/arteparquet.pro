## Competitor Categories

### 1. National Flooring Companies

Large companies with nationwide presence and marketing budgets.

**Examples:** Parquet Italia, Floor Italia chains

**Strengths:**
- Brand recognition
- Marketing resources
- Wide coverage
- Established processes

**Weaknesses:**
- Generic service
- Volume-focused
- Less personal attention
- Employee turnover

**Our Advantage:**
- Artisan quality vs. production line
- Personal attention
- La Scala credential
- Master craftsman approach

### 2. Local Contractors

Independent installers and small local companies.

**Strengths:**
- Local presence
- Personal relationships
- Often lower prices
- Quick availability

**Weaknesses:**
- Limited portfolio
- Variable quality
- No credentials
- Limited capabilities

**Our Advantage:**
- Proven track record
- Premium positioning
- National capability
- Professional presentation

### 3. DIY/Budget Options

Big box stores, discount installers.

**Strengths:**
- Lowest price
- Convenience
- Quick turnaround

**Weaknesses:**
- Quality issues
- No expertise
- Limited materials
- No design guidance

**Our Advantage:**
- Premium result
- Expert guidance
- Quality guarantee
- Lasting value
