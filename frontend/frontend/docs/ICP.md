# ICP - Ideal Customer Profile

## Arteparquet Ideal Customer Profiles

### Overview

Understanding our ideal customers allows us to craft messaging, design, and user experiences that resonate deeply and convert effectively.

---

## Primary ICPs

### ICP 1: The Affluent Homeowner

**Profile: "Maria"**

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Age** | 45-60 |
| **Gender** | Female (decision maker in home) |
| **Location** | Milan, Rome, Florence, Lake Como, Turin |
| **Income** | €80,000-200,000+ household |
| **Property** | Apartment 100-200mq or villa |
| **Status** | Married, children grown or teenagers |

**Psychographics:**
- Values quality over price
- Appreciates Italian craftsmanship
- Cares about aesthetics and design
- Wants her home to reflect her taste
- Researches thoroughly before deciding
- Asks friends and professionals for recommendations

**Project Context:**
- Renovating apartment/villa
- Replacing old flooring
- Upgrading to premium materials
- May be working with an architect or designer

**Pain Points:**
- Fear of choosing wrong material
- Worry about disruption to daily life
- Concern about hidden costs
- Difficulty finding trustworthy contractors
- Bad past experiences with tradespeople

**Goals:**
- A beautiful, warm home
- Floors that last generations
- A smooth, professional experience
- Something to be proud of

**Decision Factors:**
1. Trust and reputation
2. Quality of materials
3. Previous work examples
4. Transparent pricing
5. Personal recommendation

**How She Finds Us:**
- Google search "parquet Milano"
- Recommendation from architect/designer
- Word of mouth from friends
- Instagram inspiration

**Messaging That Resonates:**
```
"La tua casa merita l'eccellenza della Scala"
"Pavimenti che durano generazioni"
"Trasforma il tuo spazio in un'opera d'arte"
```

### ICP 2: The Architect/Interior Designer

**Profile: "Marco"**

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Age** | 35-55 |
| **Gender** | Male or Female |
| **Location** | Major Italian cities |
| **Practice** | Small to medium studio |

**Goals:**

- A partner who understands design intent
- Reliable scheduling
- Someone who makes him look good

**Decision Factors:**
1. Quality of craftsmanship
2. Reliability and punctuality
3. Technical knowledge
4. Communication quality
5. Portfolio quality

**How He Finds Us:**
- Industry reputation
- Colleague referrals
- Professional networks
- Google search for specialists
- Project case studies

**Messaging That Resonates:**
```
"Il partner che ogni architetto merita"
"Eseguiamo la tua visione con precisione millimetrica"
"Dalla Scala ai tuoi progetti più esigenti"
```

**Special Considerations:**
- B2B relationship potential
- Volume projects
- May need technical specifications
- Prefers professional-to-professional communication

3. Material quality
4. Service experience

**How He Finds Us:**
- Architect or designer referral
- Word of mouth in his network
- His property manager
- Targeted outreach

**Messaging That Resonates:**
```
"L'eccellenza riservata a chi non accetta compromessi"
"Dal Teatro alla Scala alla tua villa"
"Il pavimento della tua eredità"
```

## Anti-Personas (Who We Don't Target)

### 1. The Bargain Hunter
- Only cares about lowest price
- Will sacrifice quality for savings
- Likely to complain regardless
- Not worth the effort

### 2. The DIYer
- Wants to do it themselves
- Looking for tips, not services
- May be useful for blog traffic only

### 3. The Unrealistic Timeline
- Needs it done "yesterday"
- Unwilling to wait for quality
- Will create stress without value

## Using ICPs in Design & Content

### Homepage

| Section | Target ICP |
|---------|------------|
| Hero | All (emotional appeal) |
| Credentials | Architects, Luxury |
| Services | Homeowners |
| Portfolio | All (proof) |
| Testimonials | Match to viewer |
| CTA | All |

### Service Pages

- Homeowner focus: Benefits, transformation
- Architect focus: Technical details, reliability
- Commercial focus: Durability, timelines

### Form Questions

```
"Chi sei?"
○ Privato (homeowner ICP)
○ Architetto / Designer (architect ICP)
○ Impresa edile (construction)
○ Hotel / Ristorante (hospitality ICP)
```

This allows for personalized follow-up based on ICP.
