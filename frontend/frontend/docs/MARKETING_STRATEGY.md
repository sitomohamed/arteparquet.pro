# Marketing Strategy

## Arteparquet Marketing Plan

### Overview

This document outlines the marketing strategy to establish Arteparquet as the leading parquet specialist in Italy and generate a consistent flow of qualified leads.

---

## Marketing Objectives

### Year 1 Goals

| Objective | Target | Metric |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Brand awareness | Establish presence | Branded search volume |
| Website traffic | 5,000/month | Google Analytics |
| Lead generation | 50-100/month | Form submissions |
| Conversion rate | 20%+ leads to quotes | CRM tracking |
| Close rate | 25%+ quotes to projects | Revenue |

## Marketing Channels

### Primary Channels

#### 1. Organic Search (SEO)

**Investment:** High
**Timeline:** Long-term (6-12 months)
**Expected ROI:** Very High

**Strategy:**
- Target commercial intent keywords
- Local SEO for Italian cities
- Content marketing for informational queries
- Technical SEO excellence

**Key Actions:**
- [ ] Optimize all service pages
- [ ] Create local landing pages
- [ ] Launch blog with targeted content
- [ ] Build quality backlinks

#### 2. Google Business Profile

**Investment:** Low
**Timeline:** Immediate
**Expected ROI:** High

**Strategy:**
- Complete profile optimization
- Regular posts and updates
- Encourage client reviews
- Respond to all reviews

**Key Actions:**
- [ ] Claim and verify profile
- [ ] Add all photos
- [ ] Post weekly updates
- [ ] Request reviews post-project

#### 3. Referral Marketing

**Investment:** Low-Medium
**Timeline:** Ongoing
**Expected ROI:** Very High

**Strategy:**
- Architect partnership program
- Designer relationships
- Satisfied client referrals
- Construction company partnerships

**Key Actions:**
- [ ] Identify architect targets
- [ ] Create partnership materials
- [ ] Implement referral tracking
- [ ] Thank/reward referrers

### Secondary Channels

#### 4. Social Media (Instagram)

**Investment:** Medium
**Timeline:** Ongoing
**Expected ROI:** Medium

**Strategy:**
- Showcase portfolio visually
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Before/after reveals
- Engagement with design community

**Content Mix:**
- 50% Portfolio/projects
- 25% Process/craftsmanship
- 15% Before/after
- 10% Tips/education

#### 5. Paid Search (Google Ads)

**Investment:** Variable
**Timeline:** Immediate results
**Expected ROI:** Medium-High

**Strategy:**
- High-intent keywords only
- Geo-targeted campaigns
- Landing page optimization
- Conversion tracking

**Initial Budget:** €500-1000/month
**Target CPA:** €30-50 per lead

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

### Target CAC by Channel

| Channel | Target CAC | Notes |
|---------|------------|-------|
| Organic Search | €10-20 | Long-term investment |
| Google Ads | €30-50 | Immediate, controllable |
| Referral | €0-10 | Best quality |
| Social | €20-40 | Awareness focused |

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Average project value:** €8,000
**Referral potential:** +2 projects over time
**Estimated LTV:** €12,000-15,000

**Target CAC:LTV ratio:** 1:10 or better

## Partnership Marketing

### Architect Program

**Goal:** Become preferred parquet partner for architects

**Approach:**
1. Identify target architects in key cities
2. Create professional B2B materials
3. Offer exclusive collaboration benefits
4. Maintain relationship through quality

**Materials:**
- Professional portfolio PDF
- Technical specification sheets
- Partnership one-pager
- Reference project list

### Designer Network

**Goal:** Be recommended by interior designers

**Approach:**
1. Showcase design-forward portfolio
2. Emphasize aesthetic flexibility
3. Provide consultation for client projects
4. Co-marketing opportunities

## Review & Reputation Strategy

### Review Acquisition

**Goal:** 5+ new Google reviews per month

**Process:**
1. Complete project successfully
2. Follow up with satisfaction check
3. Send review request email
4. Make review link easy (one click)
5. Thank for review

### Review Request Template

```
Gentile [Nome],

Grazie per aver scelto Arteparquet.

Speriamo che il tuo nuovo pavimento ti stia dando 
tanta soddisfazione!

Se hai un momento, ci farebbe piacere se condividessi 
la tua esperienza con una breve recensione:

[Link Google Review]

La tua opinione aiuta altri a trovare artigiani 
di qualità.

Grazie mille!
Il Team Arteparquet
```
