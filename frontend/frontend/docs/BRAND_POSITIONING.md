# Brand Positioning

## Arteparquet Market Position

### Overview

Brand positioning defines how Arteparquet is perceived in the market relative to competitors. Our position is premium craftsman: the choice for those who demand excellence.

---

## Positioning Statement

**For** discerning homeowners, architects, and luxury property owners in Italy

**Who** need parquet installation, restoration, or premium flooring solutions

**Arteparquet is** the premier artisan flooring specialist

**That** brings Teatro alla Scala-level craftsmanship to every project

**Unlike** generic flooring contractors

**Arteparquet** delivers transformative results with 20+ years of proven excellence.

### How to Maintain Position

1. **Never compete on price**
   - Focus on value, not cost
   - Premium positioning throughout

2. **Consistent quality signals**
   - Website design
   - Communication quality
   - Portfolio curation

3. **Credential visibility**
   - La Scala always mentioned
   - Years of experience stated
   - Testimonials showcased

4. **Premium materials emphasis**
   - Quality over economy
   - Best materials only
   - No compromise messaging
