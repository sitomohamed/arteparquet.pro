# Content Strategy

## Arteparquet Content Strategy

### Overview

Content is the vehicle that delivers our brand message, builds trust, and drives conversions. Every piece of content must serve the goal of positioning Arteparquet as the premier parquet company in Italy.

---

## Content Pillars

### 1. Expertise & Authority

Establish Arteparquet as the definitive expert in parquet.

**Content Types:**
- Technical guides (How to choose parquet)
- Material comparisons (Massello vs Prefinito)
- Process explanations (How we work)
- Industry insights

### 2. Transformation & Inspiration

Show the power of beautiful floors to transform spaces.

**Content Types:**
- Portfolio showcases
- Before/After reveals
- Design inspiration
- Client stories

### 3. Trust & Credibility

Build confidence in our capabilities and reliability.

**Content Types:**
- La Scala story
- Client testimonials
- Case studies
- Behind-the-scenes

### 4. Practical Value

Provide useful information that serves the customer.

**Content Types:**
- Maintenance guides
- FAQ answers
- Pricing transparency
- Decision frameworks

## Content Quality Standards

### Writing Standards

- Clear, concise Italian
- Active voice preferred
- Benefits before features
- Specific over vague
- Scannable formatting

### Visual Standards

- High-resolution images (min 1200px wide)
- Consistent color treatment
- Professional photography
- No stock photo clichés

### SEO Standards

- Primary keyword in title
- Meta description < 160 chars
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Internal linking
- Alt text on images

---

## Content Governance

### Creation Process

1. **Brief**: Define topic, audience, goal
2. **Research**: Keywords, competitors, user needs
3. **Draft**: Write following brand voice
4. **Review**: Edit for quality and SEO
5. **Optimize**: Images, meta, links
6. **Publish**: Schedule and deploy
7. **Promote**: Social, email if applicable

### Content Maintenance

| Frequency | Task |
|-----------|------|
| Monthly | Review analytics, update underperformers |
| Quarterly | Refresh outdated content |
| Annually | Content audit, strategic review |
