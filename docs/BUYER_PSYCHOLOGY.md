## Cognitive Biases to Leverage

### 1. Authority Bias

**Principle:** People trust perceived experts

**Application:**
- La Scala credential prominently displayed
- "20+ anni di esperienza"
- Technical content showing expertise
- Industry credentials

### 2. Social Proof

**Principle:** People follow the actions of others

**Application:**
- Client testimonials
- Number of projects completed
- Client types (architects, hotels)
- Reviews and ratings

### 3. Anchoring

**Principle:** First information influences perception

**Application:**
- Lead with premium positioning
- Show best work first
- Quality before price
- La Scala as the anchor

### 4. Loss Aversion

**Principle:** Fear of loss > desire for gain

**Application:**
- "Non rischiare un lavoro mediocre"
- Emphasize what's at stake
- Show before/after of neglected floors
- Limited availability hints

### 5. Reciprocity

**Principle:** People reciprocate value received

**Application:**
- Free consultation
- Free inspection
- Helpful content
- No-obligation quotes

### 6. Commitment & Consistency

**Principle:** Small commitments lead to larger ones

**Application:**
- Multi-step form (micro-commitments)
- Newsletter signup
- Downloadable guides
- Progressive engagement

### 7. Halo Effect

**Principle:** One positive trait influences overall perception

**Application:**
- Premium design = premium service
- Beautiful portfolio = quality work
- Professional communication = reliable

## Objections & Responses

### "It's too expensive"

**Psychology:** Price vs. value calculation

**Response Approach:**
- Reframe as investment
- Emphasize longevity
- Compare to alternatives over time
- Highlight included value

**Website Implementation:**
- Quality emphasis before price
- "Built to last generations"
- Premium positioning throughout

### "I need to think about it"

**Psychology:** Fear of commitment, need for validation

**Response Approach:**
- Provide more information
- Offer reassurance
- Remove pressure
- Stay accessible

**Website Implementation:**
- Easy information access
- Multiple visits welcomed
- Remarketing (respectful)

### "I want to compare options"

**Psychology:** Risk mitigation, due diligence

**Response Approach:**
- Confidence in quality
- Differentiation clarity
- Welcome comparison
- Unique value clear

**Website Implementation:**
- Clear differentiators
- La Scala uniqueness
- Premium positioning

### "I'm worried about disruption"

**Psychology:** Fear of chaos, stress avoidance

**Response Approach:**
- Clear process explanation
- Timeline expectations
- Professional conduct promise

**Website Implementation:**
- Process section
- "Lavoriamo con rispetto"
- Professionalism signals
